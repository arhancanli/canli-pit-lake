# Community standards

Participation in canli-pit-lake should be rigorous, constructive, and safe.

- Critique methods and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Do not pressure contributors to reveal credentials, private data, or proprietary information.
- Do not manipulate, selectively omit, or misrepresent research evidence.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, or market promotion disguised as research.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when these standards are violated.
Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
